SimpleDrive - soft-clipping overdrive for the PedalPCB Terrarium
=================================================================

Platform: Daisy Seed on a PedalPCB Terrarium, Arduino IDE + DaisyDuino.
Single file: SimpleDrive.ino needs no extra headers.

CONTROLS
  Knob 1 (A1)        Drive    clean-ish boost up to about +36 dB into the clipper
  Knob 2 (A2)        Tone     post-clip lowpass, about 600 Hz to 8 kHz
  Knob 3 (A3)        Level    output volume (full = just under full scale)
  Toggle 1 (D10)     ON = asymmetric clipping (warmer, more even harmonics)
  Footswitch 1 (D25) bypass on/off, with a 10 ms crossfade (no pop)
  LED 1 (D22)        lit while the effect is on
  Knobs 4-6, toggles 2-4, footswitch 2: unused

SIGNAL FLOW
  in[0] -> drive gain -> 7 kHz pre-filter -> tanh soft clipper
        -> (asymmetric: bias + rescale) -> slow DC remover -> tone lowpass
        -> level -> bypass crossfade -> out[0] (copied to out[1])

HOW IT WORKS
  - Drive multiplies the signal, then tanh() squashes it smoothly toward +/-1.
    More gain means more of the waveform is squashed, so it sounds more distorted.
  - The 7 kHz pre-filter keeps the clipper from making harsh fizz and aliasing.
  - Asymmetric mode adds a 0.3 bias before tanh, so the top and bottom of the wave
    clip differently. The code removes the offset and rescales back to +/-1. A very
    slow (~0.8 Hz) DC remover cleans up what is left without tilting low notes.
  - Knobs are read in loop() and smoothed per sample inside the audio callback,
    so turning them makes no zipper noise.

INSTALL / UPLOAD
  1. Put SimpleDrive.ino in a folder named SimpleDrive.
  2. Arduino IDE: Tools > Board > Generic STM32H7 Series; Board part number > Daisy Seed;
     Upload method > STM32CubeProgrammer (DFU). Library: DaisyDuino.
  3. Hold BOOT on the Seed, tap RESET, release BOOT, then click Upload.

THINGS TO TRY
  - Make it louder: change 0.9f in target_level (keep it at or below 1.0f).
  - More or less gain range: change 59.0f in target_gain.
  - Different clip shape: replace tanhf(x) with x / (1.0f + fabsf(x)) (softer, cheaper).

TEST STATUS (daisyrig, --board terrarium --hw)
  Passed: no failures, compiles for the Daisy (about 56 KB flash, 43%), sanitizers clean,
  no clipping at any knob or toggle setting.
  Known: under hard drive, notes below about 150 Hz become near-square waves. The rig
  flags their edges as possible clicks. That is the distortion itself, and it sits
  below the guitar's low E (82 Hz).
